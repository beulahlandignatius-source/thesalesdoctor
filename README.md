6. UX/UI DESIGN PRINCIPLES



Apply these principles throughout the redesign:



1. Clarity: Visitors should understand what The Sales Clinic offers without struggling.

2. Consistency: Buttons, cards, typography, spacing, and icons should follow one design system.

3. Visual hierarchy: Important information should attract attention first.

4. Accessibility: Maintain readable text, sufficient contrast, keyboard navigation, and visible focus states.

5. Feedback: Buttons and forms should respond clearly to user actions.

6. Simplicity: Avoid unnecessary interactions and visual clutter.

7. Trust: Keep testimonials, evidence, disclaimers, and professional information authentic.

8. Conversion: Make the next step obvious without making the page feel aggressive.

9. Mobile-first thinking: Every section must work beautifully on smaller screens.

10. Consistency of language: Preserve the original copy and terminology.
